Course Overview

📋 Course Info
  • Course: CS 517 / PEP 557 — Quantum Information and Quantum Computation (3 Credits), Schaefer School of Engineering and Science, Fall 2026
  • Instructor: Dr. Samuel R. Hedemann (Section A) — Mondays 6:30–9:00pm, Burchard 714
  • Textbook: None required. Reference: Quantum Computation and Quantum Information, Nielsen & Chuang, 10th Anniversary Ed. (Cambridge University Press) — the course does not follow it exactly; all needed material comes from lecture notes, discussions, homework, and provided papers.
  • Prerequisites: Graduate student (or junior+ undergraduate) with a firm grasp of algebra. No prior quantum-physics background assumed — algebra and matrices are reviewed and covered in depth from lecture 1.

Description

A linear-algebra (matrix)-based course covering vector-based quantum formalism, quantum entanglement, matrices and operators, quantum teleportation, quantum logic gates, quantum circuits, quantum algorithms for quantum computing, and other applications such as quantum encryption.
