Description

An introduction to the fundamental physical, chemical, and geological processes that govern the ocean system and its role in Earth's environment. Topics include the history and importance of the ocean, seawater properties and ocean sediments, atmosphere-ocean interactions, and global ocean circulation. Students learn about ocean waves, tides, and storm surge, methods of ocean observation, estuarine and coastal processes, and the ocean's role in climate variability, global warming, and the impacts of climate change on coastal and marine systems.
