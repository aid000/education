🛠️ Hands-On Project

Build Your Personal Financial Model

Build a complete personal financial model for a realistic future self in Python (numpy/pandas/matplotlib + yfinance; Sheets as backup): a current monthly budget plus a 10-year projection under three scenarios (conservative, balanced, aggressive indexing), a simple tax estimator, a rent-vs-buy NPV calculator for a real city you care about, and one "startup equity vs index fund" scenario comparison. Deliver the model (code/spreadsheet) plus a 3–5 page "My Wealth Plan" report with risk analysis and next actions.

Wk 1: install Python + yfinance (or set up Sheets) Wk 4: budget + 10-year projection (3 scenarios) Wk 8: tax estimator + rent-vs-buy NPV Wk 10: full model + "My Wealth Plan" report

💬 Discussions

Three checkpoints — weeks 3, 6, 9

  • Week 3: Pick a budgeting system (zero-based, 50/30/20, or envelope). Why does it fit your life better than the other two?
  • Week 6: Most active funds underperform a cheap index fund over time. Why — and what does that imply for where a beginner should start?
  • Week 9: Rent vs buy for a city you care about: list the three numbers that most change the answer, and which way each one pushes.
