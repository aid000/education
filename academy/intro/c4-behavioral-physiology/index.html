🛠️ Hands-On Project

4-Week Self-Experiment + "Difficult People Playbook"

For four weeks, track daily stress triggers, energy, and mood (Python pandas/Matplotlib or a journaling app), while applying 2–3 physiology tools (physiological sigh, morning sunlight, reframing). Analyze your own data for patterns. Then write a concise "Difficult People Playbook" with 5–7 real scenarios and physiologically grounded response strategies. Deliver the tracker analysis + the playbook.

Wk 1: set up tracker (Python or journaling app) Wk 4: daily stress/mood/energy log + 2–3 tools running Wk 8: correlation analysis + first playbook scenarios Wk 10: tracker analysis + Difficult People Playbook

💬 Discussions

Three checkpoints — weeks 3, 6, 9

  • Week 3: Try the physiological sigh (double inhale, long exhale) before a stressful moment this week. What changed — and what is the vagal mechanism behind it?
  • Week 6: Loss aversion, status threat, amygdala hijack — pick one and describe a time it drove a decision you would now make differently.
  • Week 9: Describe a difficult person in your life through a physiology lens (threat / status response). Which boundary or de-escalation move fits, and why?
