🧭 Intro — Practical Life Skills for High Achievers

Five high-leverage things top high schools skip — health, work, AI, relationships, and money. Each course is a 10-week, project-based intro that turns real-world knowledge into portfolio-ready artifacts: trackers, models, prompt systems, and personal experiments. Same rigor as the technical tracks, pointed at your actual life.

🧭 How These Fit Together

Unlike the technical tracks, these five are independent — take them in any order, in parallel, or pick the ones you need now.

🎯 Why this specialization

These five domains decide an enormous amount of how life goes, yet they are almost never taught before college. The goal is not certification — it is immediately usable systems and the habit of thinking in data, mechanisms, and trade-offs. Every course produces a concrete artifact (a dashboard, a run project, a prompt system, a self-experiment, a financial model) that demonstrates initiative and execution — and pairs naturally with the technical tracks like Computational Physics and Quant Finance.

📖 Books (optional — courses are self-contained)

Lectures cover everything; these pair well for depth. C1: AAOS, Emergency Care and Transportation of the Sick and Injured. C2: Goldratt, The Goal; Buckingham & Coffman, First, Break All the Rules; the free Scrum Guide. C4: Kahneman, Thinking, Fast and Slow; Sapolsky, Why Zebras Don't Get Ulcers. C5: Bogle, The Little Book of Common Sense Investing.

⚠️ Important — educational only

This specialization is for education. The health course is exam-prep familiarization, not EMT certification or a license to treat — in a real emergency, call your local emergency number. The relationships course is not therapy. The finance course is not financial, tax, or investment advice, and any dollar figures are illustrative and dated (verify current numbers with the IRS/SSA). Always consult a qualified professional before acting.
