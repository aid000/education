🔥 Plasma Physics & Fusion Energy

From the fourth state of matter to a fusion power plant. Plasma fundamentals, kinetic theory and waves, magnetohydrodynamics, fusion reactions and confinement, and reactor concepts — every model coded in Python with PlasmaPy and friends, all on a laptop. The natural extension of the Nuclear track.

🧭 Course Progression

Take them in order. C2 (kinetic) and C3 (MHD) are the two complementary descriptions of a plasma — both build on C1.

C1 Fundamentals → C2 Kinetic & Waves ∥ C3 MHD → C4 Fusion & Confinement → C5 Reactor Concepts

🎯 Internship readiness

This sequence mirrors a university intro-plasma / fusion track — plasma physics, kinetic theory, MHD, fusion energy, and reactor concepts. It extends the Nuclear track perfectly (fission → fusion, shared OpenMC tooling) and targets internships at PPPL, Commonwealth Fusion, TAE, national labs, and private fusion startups.

📖 Books (optional)

The courses are self-contained — lectures cover everything, no required textbook. For extra depth: Chen, Introduction to Plasma Physics and Controlled Fusion (C1–C3, the classic intro); Freidberg, Plasma Physics and Fusion Energy (C4–C5).

⚠️ Modeling scope

These are reduced-order (0-D / 1-D) models — enough to build real physical intuition and reproduce textbook results on a laptop, without the multi-million-core gyrokinetic codes used in research. That scope is deliberate and clearly flagged in each project.
