🛠️ Hands-On Project

1-D MHD Equilibrium & Wave Solver (Python/SciPy)

Build two things: an equilibrium solver that takes a pressure profile and edge field and returns a self-consistent magnetic-field profile (verified against the exact θ-pinch and Bennett-pinch solutions, plus a Kruskal–Shafranov kink-stability check), and a finite-difference wave solver that numerically propagates an Alfvén wave and confirms its speed matches v_A=B/√(μ₀ρ). You deliver a short report with plots and the numeric checks from Lecture 10.

Wk 1: install Python + NumPy/SciPy Wk 4: θ-pinch equilibrium solver (SciPy ODE) Wk 8: Kruskal–Shafranov stability check added Wk 10: Bennett pinch + wave solver + report submitted

💬 Discussions

Three checkpoints — weeks 3, 6, 9

  • Week 3: If field lines are frozen into the fluid, how can a solar flare ever "reconnect" them and release stored energy? What has to break down, and why can that happen in a tiny region while the bulk plasma stays frozen?
  • Week 6: The solar wind flows outward faster than its own Alfvén speed. Can a disturbance ever propagate back upstream toward the Sun through it — and what does that imply about warning the inner solar system?
  • Week 9: The CFL condition is a property of explicit numerical schemes, not a law of physics. Why doesn't every MHD code simply switch to an implicit scheme and avoid it — what's the trade-off?
