🛠️ Hands-On Project

A Dispersion-Relation Solver & Landau-Damping Demo (PlasmaPy / NumPy / SciPy)

Build a small Python toolkit that root-finds the Bohm-Gross (Langmuir wave) dispersion relation and solves the exact kinetic dispersion relation — including Landau damping — using the plasma dispersion function. You deliver a short report with both solvers, a plot overlaying the fluid and kinetic real parts and the damping rate, and a written reconciliation of where the two pictures agree and where the fluid model is structurally blind. Carry over your Plasma & Fusion C1 Python + PlasmaPy environment (a quick re-check is included in Lecture 1).

Wk 1: confirm Python + PlasmaPy + NumPy/SciPy environment Wk 4: Bohm-Gross root-finder Wk 8: exact kinetic (Landau-damping) solver Wk 10: report + reconciliation submitted

💬 Discussions

Three checkpoints — weeks 3, 6, 9

  • Week 3: Linearization drops the term (e/m)E₁·∂f₁/∂v as "second order small." What has to happen to f₀ itself once a wave's amplitude grows too large to ignore that term?
  • Week 6: A wave can only reach plasma regions below its own critical density. What does that imply about where a given frequency deposits its energy in a non-uniform plasma, and how could you use reflected frequencies to infer a density profile?
  • Week 9: Landau damping and the two-stream instability come from the same resonance mechanism with opposite signs of ∂f₀/∂v. Is it fair to call them "two phenomena," or one process with two outcomes depending on f₀'s shape?
