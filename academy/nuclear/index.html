⚛️ Nuclear

Nuclear power plants & reactor engineering, from fission physics to plant operations. Neutron interactions, reactor neutronics, thermal cycles, full reactor systems, and operations & safety — every concept simulated on a laptop with free software.

🧭 Course Progression

Take them in order. C2 and C3 can run in parallel — both only require C1.

C1 Nuclear Physics → C2 Reactor Physics ∥ C3 Thermo & Heat → C4 Reactor Systems → C5 Ops & Safety

🎯 Internship readiness

This sequence mirrors a typical university nuclear-engineering track — intro nuclear engineering, neutron physics, reactor theory, thermal-hydraulics, and nuclear power systems. The AP foundation plus basic Python is all you need to begin; no extra prerequisites.

📖 Books (optional)

The courses are self-contained — lectures cover everything, no required textbook. For extra depth these classics pair well: Lamarsh & Baratta, Introduction to Nuclear Engineering (C1–C2); Duderstadt & Hamilton, Nuclear Reactor Analysis (C2); a standard thermodynamics & heat-transfer text (C3).

⚠️ Software to verify (*)

RELAP5-3D access may be gated (export-controlled / licensed). During the C5 build we confirm availability; if it's not freely obtainable on a laptop, C5's project falls back to an open Python thermal-hydraulics model. Everything else in the list is openly downloadable.
