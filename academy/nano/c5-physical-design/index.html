🛠️ Hands-On Project

Large Block: Floorplan → Route → Signoff (OpenROAD)

Take a sizable block (e.g. a pipeline stage) through the full backend — floorplan, power grid, placement, CTS, routing, and signoff — optimizing timing and power, and deliver a full signoff report (timing/IR/DRC/LVS results).

Wk 1: install OpenROAD/OpenLane (Docker) Wk 5: floorplan + placement done Wk 10: routed + signoff report

💬 Discussions

Three checkpoints — weeks 3, 6, 9

  • Week 3: Placement looks like "just arranging cells," yet it largely decides whether a chip can be routed and meet timing — why does this early step have such outsized downstream impact?
  • Week 6: Why must timing be signed off across many process/voltage/temperature corners and operating modes, instead of one nominal condition — and what fails if you don't?
  • Week 9: Late fixes use metal-only ECOs with pre-placed spare cells rather than re-running the whole design — why, and what are the trade-offs?
