🛠️ Hands-On Project

Simulate a CMOS Process Flow (OpenLane + Sky130 PDK)

Walk a simple standard cell through the open-source Sky130 process: inspect the layer stack, run the relevant process / layout steps, and document how each fabrication step (oxidation, doping, deposition, etch) maps to a layer in the final layout. Deliver a report relating physical process to the resulting structure.

Wk 3: install OpenLane + Sky130 (Docker) Wk 7: trace a cell's layer stack Wk 10: process→layout report

💬 Discussions

Three checkpoints — weeks 3, 6, 9

  • Week 3: A chip is built from dozens of stacked thin films — why does the quality and order of these early layers (e.g. the gate oxide) matter so much for what happens downstream?
  • Week 6: Diffusion vs. ion implantation for doping — what trade-offs drove industry toward implantation?
  • Week 9: Where does yield loss come from, and why does it dominate the economics of a fab?
