Fundamentals & wafers · Weeks 1–2
1. Doping silicon with phosphorus (group V) produces…
Answer: A. A group-V donor adds a free electron → n-type.
2. Silicon's ~1.1 eV band gap means it behaves as…
Answer: C. A band gap of ~1.1 eV is too large for a metal yet small enough to switch conduction on and off — the defining trait of a semiconductor.
3. Electronic-grade silicon is purified to about…
Answer: C. Electronic-grade silicon reaches "nine-nines" (9N) purity — better than one stray atom per billion.
4. A single-crystal silicon boule is grown by…
Answer: A. The Czochralski process pulls a rotating seed crystal from molten silicon to grow a single-crystal boule.
Deposition & lithography · Weeks 3–4
5. Reacting silicon with oxygen to grow SiO₂ is…
Answer: A. Thermal oxidation grows SiO₂ by reacting oxygen with the wafer, consuming some of the underlying silicon.
6. CVD forms a film by…
Answer: A. Chemical vapor deposition reacts precursor gases at the wafer surface to build up a solid film.
7. Sputtering is…
Answer: C. Sputtering is a physical vapor deposition (PVD) technique that knocks atoms off a target, commonly to deposit metals.
8. In POSITIVE photoresist, the light-exposed regions…
Answer: A. In positive resist, exposure makes the illuminated regions soluble, so they wash away in develop.
9. The minimum printable feature size scales with…
Answer: B. By the Rayleigh criterion, resolution ∝ λ/NA — shorter wavelength and higher numerical aperture print smaller features.
10. EUV lithography uses a wavelength of about…
Answer: C. Extreme ultraviolet lithography operates at ~13.5 nm, far shorter than the 193 nm deep-UV it replaced.
Etching & doping · Weeks 5–6
11. Wet (liquid) etching is generally…
Answer: B. Liquid etchants attack in all directions (isotropic), so they undercut beneath the mask edge.
12. Vertical sidewalls on very small features require…
Answer: C. Directional (anisotropic) dry / reactive-ion etching cuts straight down, giving the vertical sidewalls small features need.
13. In ion implantation, the depth of the dopants is set mainly by…
Answer: B. Higher ion energy drives dopants deeper; dose sets the amount, energy sets the depth.
14. Immediately after an implant you must…
Answer: C. A post-implant anneal heals the lattice damage from the bombardment and moves dopants onto active lattice sites.
15. Ion implantation is preferred over diffusion mainly for…
Answer: B. Implantation independently tunes dose (beam current) and depth (energy), giving far tighter control than thermal diffusion.
Transistor & interconnect · Weeks 7–8
16. The thin insulator under a MOSFET's gate is the…
Answer: D. The gate oxide is the thin insulating layer that capacitively couples the gate to the channel below.
17. "Self-aligned" source/drain means…
Answer: B. The gate itself blocks the implant over the channel, so the source/drain regions automatically align to the gate edges.
18. CMOS logic requires…
Answer: D. Complementary MOS pairs n-MOS and p-MOS devices (in p-well and n-well), which is why it draws almost no static power.
19. Copper interconnect is formed by…
Answer: B. Copper is hard to etch, so the damascene flow etches a trench in dielectric, fills it with copper, then planarizes by CMP.
20. Vertical connections between metal layers are called…
Answer: A. Vias are the metal-filled vertical plugs that connect one interconnect layer to the next.
21. Low-k dielectrics are used to…
Answer: D. A lower dielectric constant between wires cuts the line-to-line capacitance, reducing RC delay and crosstalk.
Test, yield & scaling · Weeks 9–10
22. Yield is defined as…
Answer: D. Yield is the fraction of working dies — good dies divided by total dies on the wafer.
23. Yield dominates chip cost because…
Answer: D. You pay to process the whole wafer regardless, so the cost per working die scales as 1/yield.
24. Short-channel effects cause…
Answer: D. As channels shrink, the source/drain fields encroach and the gate loses control, raising off-state leakage.
25. A FinFET improves on the planar MOSFET by…
Answer: D. The FinFET's gate wraps the thin fin on three sides, restoring electrostatic control and curbing short-channel leakage.
