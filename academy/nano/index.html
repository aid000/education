🔬 Nano

Semiconductor & chip design, from AP Physics C to tape-out. Master the physical fabrication process and the logical CPU architecture, then integrate both in a complete open-source VLSI flow — internship-ready for a Micron / Nvidia-type role.

🧭 Course Progression

Take them in order. C2 and C3 can run in parallel — both only require C1.

C1 Circuits → C2 Fabrication ∥ C3 Architecture → C4 VLSI → C5 Physical Design

📚 Courses

Each course is 10 weekly lectures + weekly MCQ quizzes + one project + a final exam.

🎯 Internship readiness

This 5-course sequence mirrors a typical university nanofab / chip-design track — fundamentals, fabrication, architecture, VLSI, and backend physical design. Completing all five with the projects leaves you tape-out-aware and ready for a backend-VLSI internship.

📖 Books (optional)

The courses are self-contained — lectures cover everything you need, no required textbook. If you want extra depth, these classics pair well: Sedra & Smith, Microelectronic Circuits (C1); Sze, Semiconductor Devices: Physics and Technology (C2); Harris & Harris, Digital Design and Computer Architecture (C3); Weste & Harris, CMOS VLSI Design (C4–C5).
