🛠️ Hands-On Project

RISC-V Core Subset: RTL → Layout (OpenLane)

Take a small RISC-V core subset from Verilog RTL through synthesis, floorplan, place, CTS, route, and signoff to a GDSII layout, checking timing with STA along the way. Deliver a report with the flow results and timing/area numbers.

Wk 1: install OpenLane + Yosys + KLayout (Docker) Wk 5: RTL written + simulated Wk 10: RTL→GDSII + STA report

💬 Discussions

Three checkpoints — weeks 3, 6, 9

  • Week 3: Verilog looks like software but describes hardware that all runs at once — why is the "parallel hardware" mindset essential, and what bugs appear when you write it like C?
  • Week 6: Synthesis turns the same RTL into very different chips depending on constraints — what does it actually do, and why can't you judge RTL quality without the constraints and target library?
  • Week 9: Place-and-route is automated yet remains one of the hardest steps — why is routing so hard, and why can a logically correct netlist still fail because of it?
