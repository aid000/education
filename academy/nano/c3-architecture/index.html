💬 Discussions

Three checkpoints — weeks 3, 6, 9

  • Week 3: What can a circuit WITH memory (sequential logic) do that pure combinational logic cannot — and why does a CPU need it?
  • Week 6: Hardwired vs microcoded control — what does each optimize for, and why did complex (CISC) machines favor microcode while modern RISC pipelines lean hardwired?
  • Week 9: If SRAM is so much faster than DRAM, why don't we build all of main memory from SRAM?
