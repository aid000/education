States, Amplitudes & the Born Rule · Weeks 1–2
1. For a qubit |ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩, the normalization condition is…
Answer: A. The squared magnitudes of the amplitudes must sum to 1 so that the Born-rule probabilities sum to 1.
2. For |φ⟩ = a|0⟩+b|1⟩ and |ψ⟩ = c|0⟩+d|1⟩, the inner product ⟨φ|ψ⟩ equals…
Answer: C. The bra's coefficients are complex-conjugated before multiplying: ⟨φ|ψ⟩ = a*c + b*d.
3. A qubit is in |ψ⟩ = √0.2 |0⟩ + √0.8 |1⟩ (real, positive coefficients). The probability of measuring 1 is…
Answer: B. P(1) = |√0.8|² = 0.8, by the Born rule.
4. The bra ⟨ψ| associated with a ket |ψ⟩ is…
Answer: D. ⟨ψ| ≡ |ψ⟩† — star every amplitude and turn the column into a row. It is used to form inner products and outer products (density operators).
Operators & Measurement · Week 3
5. An operator A is called Hermitian when…
Answer: B. Hermitian means equal to its own conjugate transpose, A† = A — the defining property of a valid observable operator.
6. The eigenvalues of a Hermitian operator are always…
Answer: D. Hermiticity guarantees real eigenvalues — essential, since eigenvalues are the possible outcomes of a physical measurement.
7. The expectation value of observable A in state |ψ⟩ is written…
Answer: A. Sandwiching the operator between the bra and ket of the state gives the mean value of repeated measurements of A.
8. Immediately after a projective measurement yields eigenvalue λ, the state is…
Answer: C. Measurement collapses the state onto the eigenstate (or eigenspace) belonging to the observed eigenvalue λ.
Bloch Sphere & Unitary Evolution · Weeks 4–5
9. On the Bloch sphere, the state |0⟩ sits at…
Answer: C. By convention |0⟩ is the north pole and |1⟩ the south pole; superpositions live on the surface between them.
10. A unitary operator U satisfies…
Answer: A. Unitarity (U†U = I) is exactly what preserves the norm of a state under evolution, keeping total probability equal to 1.
11. The Pauli-X gate acts on the Bloch sphere as…
Answer: D. X is the quantum NOT gate — a π rotation about the Bloch x-axis that exchanges the north and south poles.
12. Unitary time evolution preserves which quantity of a quantum state?
Answer: B. Unitary operators preserve the norm of the state vector, so total measurement probability stays exactly 1 at every instant — the state's position on the Bloch sphere generally does move.
13. The time-evolution operator U(t) = e^(−iHt/ħ) is guaranteed to be unitary because…
Answer: C. Exponentiating i times a Hermitian operator always produces a unitary operator — this is exactly why the Hamiltonian must be Hermitian in the first place.
14. Which of these is unobservable, having no effect on any measurement probability?
Answer: A. A global phase leaves every |amplitude|² unchanged, so it has zero physical effect; the relative phase between amplitudes, by contrast, is physically real.
Multi-Qubit Systems & Entanglement · Weeks 6–7
15. A general n-qubit state requires how many complex amplitudes?
Answer: D. Each additional qubit doubles the joint space's dimension — exponential, not linear or polynomial, growth.
16. Given |ψ⟩=|1⟩ and |φ⟩=(|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2, the tensor product |ψ⟩⊗|φ⟩ equals…
Answer: B. With α=0,β=1 for |ψ⟩ and γ=δ=1/√2 for |φ⟩: αγ|00⟩+αδ|01⟩+βγ|10⟩+βδ|11⟩ = 0+0+(1/√2)|10⟩+(1/√2)|11⟩.
17. A two-qubit state is entangled exactly when…
Answer: C. Entanglement is defined purely by the impossibility of factoring the joint state into independent single-qubit descriptions.
18. Which of the following is not one of the four Bell states?
Answer: A. |00⟩ is an ordinary, unentangled product state (|0⟩⊗|0⟩). B, C, and D are the Bell states Φ⁺, Ψ⁻, and Φ⁻ respectively.
19. When Alice measures her half of an entangled Bell pair, the effect on Bob's distant qubit is…
Answer: D. The correlation is real and instantaneous in the sense of the joint state, but Alice cannot encode a message this way — no information travels faster than light.
20. Experimentally observed violations of Bell's inequality show that…
Answer: B. The measured correlations match the quantum prediction and exceed the bound any local hidden-variable theory allows, ruling that class of explanation out.
Density Matrices, No-Cloning & Capstone · Weeks 8–10
21. The density operator for a pure state |ψ⟩ is defined as…
Answer: A. ρ is the outer product of the ket with its own bra, a matrix representation of the same state.
22. Which is NOT one of the three required properties of a valid density matrix?
Answer: C. Off-diagonals encode coherence/superposition and are allowed (even expected) to be nonzero; only Hermiticity, trace 1, and positivity are required.
23. The proof that no unitary can clone an arbitrary unknown qubit relies crucially on unitary operators being…
Answer: B. Linearity forces a would-be cloning unitary to distribute over a superposition term-by-term, producing an entangled state instead of the desired product state |ψ⟩⊗|ψ⟩.
24. Tracing out one qubit of the Bell state |Φ⁺⟩ leaves the other qubit in the state…
Answer: D. Even though the full two-qubit state is pure, the reduced single-qubit state after tracing out its entangled partner is maximally mixed — zero coherence remains.
25. The standard two-gate recipe for preparing a Bell state from |00⟩, used throughout the capstone, is…
Answer: C. Hadamard creates the superposition on qubit A; CNOT then correlates qubit B's value with A's, producing an entangled Bell state from an unentangled product state.
