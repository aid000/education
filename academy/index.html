🧩 How Every Course Works

One consistent template across all specializations.

📅

10 Weeks, 10 Lectures

Each course is ten weekly lectures delivered as slides + reading, building cumulatively toward the final.

❓

MCQ Quiz Each Week

5–10 multiple-choice questions after every lecture, with answer keys and explanations.

🛠️

Hands-On Project

One project per course using free, laptop-only software. Built across the 10 weeks, report due week 10.

💬

3 Discussions

Discussion prompts at weeks 3, 6, and 9 to reflect and connect ideas across units.

📝

Final Exam

One comprehensive final, no midterm. Tests theory plus applied simulation problems.

⚖️

Transparent Grading

Quizzes 30% · Project 30% · Final 30% · Discussions 10%. Same weighting in every course.
